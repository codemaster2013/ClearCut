2. It cuts out automaticallyClearcut learns the colours of the backdrop and the subject, then follows the real edge. No button to press.

Private by design

Your photo is processed on your own device. Clearcut has no server for images, no account and no tracking, and it works offline after the first visit.

Questions

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?No. The whole cut-out runs inside your browser. Your photo never leaves your device, and there is no account or sign-up.
Is it really free, with no watermark?Yes. There are no credits, no limits and no watermark on the PNG you download.
Does it use an AI model?No. Clearcut uses its own cut-out engine written from scratch. It learns the colours of the background and of the subject from your photo, then follows the real edge of the subject. It does not use a pretrained neural network or any outside code.
When does it work best, and when does it struggle?It works best when the subject's colours are different from the background, such as products on a plain surface or people against a wall. Busy backgrounds, similar colours, hair and glass can need a few brush strokes. Use the Erase brush on leftover background and the Restore brush to bring back parts of the subject.
Can I use it offline?Yes. After your first visit the site is stored on your device, so you can install it and cut out photos without internet.
What size can the result be?The editor works at up to 2048 pixels on the longest side. If your photo is larger you can export at its original size, using your original pixels with the edited transparency.
